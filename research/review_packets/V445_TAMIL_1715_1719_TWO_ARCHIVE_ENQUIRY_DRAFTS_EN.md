## 1. Württembergische Landesbibliothek — old prints and Bible collection

**To:** ad@wlb-stuttgart.de  
**Subject:** Bibliographical enquiry: Tamil New Testament 1715, shelfmark Ba Indien 1715 01

Dear Special Collections Team,

I am preparing a non-commercial historical research overview on early Bible translation and printing in South India. Your published list *Frühe Missionsbibeln: Wichtige Stationen* identifies a Tamil New Testament (Ziegenbalg, Tranquebar Mission) dated 1715 under shelfmark **Ba Indien 1715 01**. The Bible Society of India's exhibition text, however, describes a complete Tamil New Testament published in **1719**.

I do not want to infer an edition history that the surviving objects cannot support. Could you kindly advise on the following?

1. Does your 1715 item contain the complete New Testament (all books), and how is the completeness established: actual text collation, catalogue description or another source?
2. What imprint date(s), colophon, pagination/quire sequence, and authorship information appear in the item? If available, could you indicate a stable catalogue link or permitted reference to a digital image of the title page and the last leaf?
3. Are you aware of a distinct 1719 Tamil New Testament edition, issue, completion milestone or later institutional dating convention that might explain the discrepancy? If not, a statement that this falls outside your records would also be very useful.

The purpose is to cite each institution accurately and explicitly retain any unresolved discrepancy. I would be grateful for a shelfmark, catalogue entry or bibliographic source rather than a copyrighted scan if licensing is uncertain.

## 2. Bible Society of India — archives and historical exhibition

**To:** info@biblesociety.in  
**Subject:** Source enquiry: Tamil New Testament 'published in 1719' in your Bible exhibition

Dear Archives and Exhibition Team,

I am researching the publication history of the first Tamil New Testament printed through the Tranquebar mission. On your official *BSI Archives* exhibition page, the text accompanying the **1714 first-part title page** states that the entire Tamil New Testament was published in **1719**. Meanwhile, the Württembergische Landesbibliothek lists a 1715 Tamil New Testament associated with Ziegenbalg (shelfmark Ba Indien 1715 01).

Could you please help us distinguish these records?

1. What historical bibliography, title page, colophon, edition identifier or object record supports the **1719** date in the exhibition description?
2. Is 1719 intended to identify the first complete text, a later edition, a first separate binding or another bibliographical milestone?
3. Does your archive hold an item specifically dated 1719, or could you refer us to a catalogue or holding library that does?

We will keep the difference explicitly unresolved until the records can be reconciled. We only seek a source reference or a link to an existing publicly accessible description; please do not send material with uncertain reuse rights.

Thank you for preserving and documenting this history.

Kind regards,

[Researcher name / affiliation to be supplied by sender]

**Sources consulted:** [BSI exhibition text](https://www.bsind.org/bsi-archives/); [BSI official contact](https://www.bsind.org/contact-us/).
