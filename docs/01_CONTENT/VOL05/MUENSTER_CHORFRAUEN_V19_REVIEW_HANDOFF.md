## Documentary research progress

- **1649-02-25** conditional civic permission, Stadtarchiv Münster A II 20 Bd79 fol5v (Jakobi 1998 scholarly citation), target **APW III D1 pp237–238**, Helmut Lahrkamp ed. *Stadtmünsterische Akten und Vermischtes* (1964).
- **1649-03-03** guild opposition, A II 20 Bd79 fol13v. Opposition is **not evidence of permanent expulsion**.
- **1649-09-20** one-year provisional stay compromise reported in textual search excerpt of Simone Laqua-O'Donnell 2014 book from an **unofficial mirror**; not publisher-certified or checked against the council original.
- **1653-07-04**, a reported **Revers** documented by *Westfälisches Klosterbuch*, ed. Karl Hengst, Münster 1994, digitally republished 2024. Restrictions included **twelve sisters**, permission to acquire one house but prohibition on further land, inheritance waiver by local entrants, a German secular confessor, papal confirmation relating to further mendicant houses. Direct 1653 document/shelfmark NOT FOUND. The physical act is not proven identical with a distinct permanent settlement authorization.
- **1655** convent property at Hörstertor acquired (Münster city official 2004 history + monastery encyclopedia); **1661** later extension, not to be conflated with 1661 corporate guild suppression.
- **1642** physical arrival in official city / lexicon; **Jakobi c1643** scholarly approximation; **1644** school opened. Keep as separate source-specific milestones.
