## Concrete historical findings

The historic church/municipal guild conflict over the Lorraine refugee education sisters (Congregatio Beatae Mariae Virginis, Lotharinger Chorfrauen) immediately after 1648 was documented in actual **dated city-council records**, according to the fully accessible 1998 scholarly article by **Franz-Josef Jakobi**, *Westphaliae Metropolis Monasterium*. Its precise references are:

- **1649-02-25** city council conditional approval with restraints, pope's authority and Gesamtgilde consent: Stadtarchiv Münster **A II 20 Bd.79 fol.5v**. Jakobi n.42 cites text published in Helmut Lahrkamp, **APW III D1 pp.237–238** (critical edition actual page not read).
- **1649-03-03** rejection from guild masters discussed in city council: **A II 20 Bd.79 fol.13v** (Jakobi n.43).
- **1647-04-08** Prince Bishop Ferdinand's earlier permissive mandate from Bonn: **A VIII 226 Beilage**, archival copy reference Jakobi n.44.
- **Gesamtgilde Rotes Buch**: **A XI 53**, historical guild corporation statute book (Jakobi n.50). NOT Kramergilde admission book 1662–1797.

The central actors were both **Catholic** church representatives and civic guilds; the question involved **settlement of religious teaching women**, the rise of exempt property and labor/guard/tax obligations, and the constitutional powers of guild representatives. It is **not** a named craft guild member's religious exclusion judgment under **IPO V §35**.

Jakobi also reports **1589-11-12** the Krameramtshaus inauguration celebration had **82 guild members and 24 widows of deceased masters**. **Widow attendance is not independent legal guild admission** and does not prove **1670 Maria Schürman** was admitted in her own right. This is an important correction and additional social-context lead.

Jakobi describes resistance into summer/autumn 1649; **do not extrapolate to permanent expulsion or final denial** without later direct contemporary source records. The 2014 Simone Laqua-O'Donnell Oxford university-press monograph chapter's **abstract only** was inspected; book full chapter and individual footnotes were NOT read.
