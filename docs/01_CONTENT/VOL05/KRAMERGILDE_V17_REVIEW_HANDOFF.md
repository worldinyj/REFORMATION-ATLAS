## What actually changed

- **Two additional person candidates:** Sendenhorst Heimatverein cites **Josef Ketteler 1931** under local merchants: **1670 Maria Schürman** (“Wwe Kramer Johann Schwicker aus Sendenhorst”) and **1720 Gerhard Henrich Bußman** (“aus Sendenhorst”). Website is local secondary excerpt; it gives no exact article page. **DO NOT declare Maria personally admitted**. Wwe = widow, and the list could record a deceased merchant's household or relationship rather than her individual guild entry.
- **Previously verified named person:** **1719 Anton Reinhard Neuhaus**, Amtsohn admitted to Kramergilde according to Saxon Academy person biography citing Ketteler **p270**. Its original handwritten admission and exact 1931 printed p270 image remain unread.
- **New archival lead:** Max Geisberg, *Die Stadt Münster*, part III, *Die Bürgerhäuser und Adelshöfe bis zum Jahre 1700* (1934), roughly **p256**, cites **“Gildebuch der Kramergilde im Stadtarchive”** within 1589 Krameramtshaus building history. Full printed image inaccessible via web screenshot. **Not proven identical** to Ketteler's 1662–1797 membership book.
- **Distinguish another guild book:** Official Münster 1648 congress exhibition inventories **“Das Rote Buch der Gesamtgilde” ca1410, revised 1565**, a *different city guild confederation*. Also a 1598 guild arms drawing, 1608 Kramer beer vessel, 1934 historic house photograph — **metadata only, no images downloaded or rights claimed**.
- Paderborn University digitized Geisberg 1934 and shows a CC BY 4.0 notice on this online reproduction. This notice does **not** clear images of the original medieval manuscript, separate museum artifacts or later 1931 Ketteler source.
