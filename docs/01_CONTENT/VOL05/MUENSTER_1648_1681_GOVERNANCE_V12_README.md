## Historical substance

The 1648 Westphalian settlement did not erase urban-power struggles. This draft carefully separates the **1657 first siege** and 21 October compromise from the July 1660 – 26 March 1661 **second siege and capitulation**. Weidner's 2000 scholarly monograph documents the 1661-08-19 removal of the older council and replacement by loyal Catholic lifetime appointees; a judge's greater authority over city citizen-admissions; **dissolution of Gesamtgilde** and supervision of Einzelgilden; a successful named **Greiteke Brinkmans** admission in 1677 (Gimpel 1982 secondary printed transcription); and 1681-10-10 **limited, revocable council franchise**, entered 13 October.
