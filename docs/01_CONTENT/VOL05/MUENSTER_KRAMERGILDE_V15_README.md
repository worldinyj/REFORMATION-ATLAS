## Verified new documentary insights

1. Correct **Josef Ketteler**, *Die Aufnahmen in die Kramergilde 1662–1797* (not 1662–1779), in *Quellen und Forschungen zur Geschichte der Stadt Münster*, vol. 4 (1931), **pp. 253–295** in official 2025 Historical Commission contents. The official bibliography identifies an **Internet Archive scan of combined vol. 3+4** (https://archive.org/details/quellen-und-forschungen-zur-geschichte-der-stadt-muenster-vol-3-vol-4), but the current web reader failed to open it. DO NOT assert printed pages or underlying 17th/18th-century guild lists were directly read.
2. **Actual admitted person, 1719:** Saxon Academy of Sciences Gottsched correspondence biographical register references **Anton Reinhard Neuhaus / Niehauß / Niehues**, member in **1719 as Amtsohn**, citing **Ketteler 1931 p.270**. Merchant linked secondarily to Prinzipalmarkt 48, 1720–1760; exact person-address link requires corroboration. No denomination recorded, no rejected application, no IPO Article V §35 dispute.
3. **Civic oath and timing**: Klaus Gimpel (1982), *Neubürger der Stadt Münster 1661–1815*, reports citizenship oath required for guild membership. Guild entry and treasury registers can differ by **up to two years**. City's treasury accounting series cited **StdAM A VIII 277**. **1777 eight 'Neukramer'** in the city accounts do NOT prove the complete 1777 admissions cohort.
4. **Corporate and material-history timeline**: official city source says **Krameramtshaus built 1589** and housed the Dutch delegation in **1648**. University history says guild corporation legally abolished **1810-02-20**; **142 members as of 1810** reported retrospectively in 1823; last former guild meeting **1823-06-12** and guild house sale **1824-04-12**. Publication series end **1797** is not legal dissolution date **1810**.
