# VOL05 V1.4 — Münster Bakers Guild: Real Archival Record before 1661

**DRAFT_RESEARCH_NOT_PUBLIC** · 2026-10-29 · `research/vol05-bakers-guild-primary-leads-v14`

## Verified new catalog record

- **1639-04-04 Bäckeramtsrolle Münster**, LWL DWUD **regest 112746**; Landesarchiv NRW Westfalen, **Verein für Geschichte und Altertumskunde Westfalens (Dep.) — Urkunden Nr.1527**. A **paper booklet copy** and later annotations, **NOT 3 physical original documents**.
- The catalog lists 33 guild rules including apprenticeships, breadmaking, guild master elections, admission and funeral cross roles in rules 26–30.
- Within same booklet: **1660-01-12** city council allows/conditions free guild-master elections with one-off **600 Reichstaler** and **1660-01-18** guild members decide the election procedure.
- A **1641-09-23/27** Bernd Beckmann dispute concerns miller descent, not a proved Protestant exclusion; pre-1648 and cannot be IPO V §35 legal enforcement.
- **1661-08-22** princely guild ordinance is documented in Deter 1999 via **Franz Bischof, 1926, p.22**. The actual 1661 archival manuscript identifier remains unknown; the 1926 page itself is not read.
- **Bischof 1926** bibliographic verification: *Der Anteil der Gilden am Stadtregiment in den westfälischen Städten*, Warendorf: Darpe, 82pp, old Paderborn bibliography EAB **41,727** (not an online manuscript link or current borrowing confirmation).
- Independent **1718-02-18** guild jurisdiction rescript LWL regest **113079**, **Urkunden Nr.1861**, has later 1790 appeal/copy provenance. It is not a 17th century IPO V35 lawsuit.
