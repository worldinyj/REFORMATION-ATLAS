## Material source discoveries

1. **MLA resolved**: Weidner 2000 *Landadel in Münster 1600–1760* refers to **Münstersches Landesarchiv** (an older prince-bishopric/state archive grouping). Scholar's original item locator **MLA 413–5, fol. 126r–128r** for 1661 town judge instruction remains a **cited shelfmark**; its photographed folios have NOT been read. Matching this with the distinct **LWL eighteenth-century copy Verein Urkunden Nr.1626** is unresolved.
2. **Gimpel's 1982 footnote 16** explicitly records the location of the **Greiteke Brinkmans 1677-05-15 Bürgerbrief**: **“StdAM, Stiftungsarchiv Armenhaus Zumbusch unverzeichnet”**. *Unverzeichnet* is 1982 *unlisted/unregistered* status, not proof of current absence or current individual identifier.
3. **Modern source collection exists**: Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek now lists **Stadtarchiv Münster C-Arm Zumbusch / Armenhaus Zumbusch-Üding**, including **Akten Nr.28** (1661–1670 accounts) and **Urk. Nr.37** (1412 legal transaction). **Neither item is the 1677 civic letter** and their signatures MUST NOT be reused for it.
4. Historical background: municipal Zumbusch foundation founded 1337 near Martini church and later housed women; these facts do NOT prove Greiteke lived there. The foundation's 1930 photo is not a historical depiction of the 1677 Bürgerbrief.
5. Gimpel's description of the citizen letter as two paper leaves and with a paper seal remains **secondary published witness description**, not inspected manuscript original. No reliable individual record currently proves IPO Article V §35 litigation or religious denial of Greiteke's admission.
