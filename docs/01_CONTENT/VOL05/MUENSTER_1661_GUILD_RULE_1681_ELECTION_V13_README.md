## New historical evidence

- **1661-08-22 guild rule**: Christoph Bernhard von Galen's regulation addressed *Alder- und Meisterleute* and curtailed the **Gesamtgilde** political federation, required permission for assemblies, loyalty oath/approval for guild elders and records submitted to the town judge. Full **Deter 1999** scholarly HTML was read; it attributes a short historic regulation excerpt to **Franz Bischof 1926, p.22**. This is **indirectly reported contemporaneous text, not a first-hand original source reading**. Original guild rule shelfmark UNKNOWN.
- **1681-10-10 election edict**: **Marcus Weidner 2000 ch.2 printed p.135 n.81** specifies Münster **AA II 0 fol.96r** as the election decree. Same election book records last preinterruption election **1661-01-18 fol.92v/93r**. Full text of the election grant inserted in council minutes **1681-10-13**. At most academic text extraction, not individual archival scans. City Museum Münster independently confirms **1681 return of elections with continued prince-bishop judicial control**.
- **1690-08-30 guild jurisdiction**: Deter reports Münster municipal magistrates gained first-instance crafts dispute authority, indicating continued guild influence **without** treating political guild rights as wholly restored. The 1731 imperial craft reform and 1732 territorial implementation remain a later legal context.
- **1536/1553**: guild political exclusion after Anabaptist rule and subsequent restoration are antecedent and must never be attributed to the 1648 treaty.
