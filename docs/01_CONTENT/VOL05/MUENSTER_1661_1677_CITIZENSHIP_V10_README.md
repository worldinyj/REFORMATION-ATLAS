Indexed scholarly PDF excerpts discuss witness testimony, Catholic upbringing, magistrate Johann Dietrich Rave, and civic oath.

3. **Council change locator**: Weidner monograph p.130 n.57 cites **AA II 23, RP 19.08.1661 (Vorform)** for Catholic lifetime council appointments. A scholarly footnote is not firsthand direct manuscript inspection.
4. **Visual context candidate**: Library of Congress *Assedio della Citta di Mvnster Anno 1661*, rights advisory **No known restrictions on publication**. Art depicts the **1661 siege**, NOT **1677 citizenship ceremony**. Metadata only; no image downloaded or published.
